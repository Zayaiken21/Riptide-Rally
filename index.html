Items

Drive through a glowing box to get a random item. Racers near the back get the big ones.

Course hazards

Whirlpool (every course): pulls in any boat that strays inside the swirl, drags it under, then launches it backwards behind the whirlpool. Boost to fight the pull.
Kelp beds (Palm Key): thick weed that cuts your speed while you are in it.
Geysers (Coral Canyon): bubble for a moment, then erupt and throw boats into the air.
Swinging booms (Lantern Bay): floating logs that swing out across the course and bump boats aside.
Underwater tunnels (Sunken Reef, Aurora Fjord): the course dives under the sea through a glass tunnel. Item boxes and boost pads are down there too.
Jellyfish (Sunken Reef): drift across the tunnels and sting any boat that touches them into a spin.
Icebergs and ice (Aurora Fjord): icebergs drift across the course and bump boats aside; slick ice patches make your boat slide.
